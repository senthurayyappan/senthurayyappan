I'm a PhD student in Robotics at the **University of Michigan**, working with Professor
Elliott Rouse in the [Neurobionics Lab](https://neurobionics.robotics.umich.edu/). My
research is on **robot codesign**: how a robot's mechanical design and its control policy
can co-evolve inside simulation, instead of being engineered one after the other. Before
the PhD I spent 2021–2025 leading the Open-Source Leg ecosystem, whose hardware and
software stack is now used by more than 25 research groups worldwide.

Latest from the blog — [**The Ballbot Always Wins**](https://senthurayyappan.com/blog/ballbot-always-wins):
the origin story of the ROB311 robot that balances on top of a basketball.

Every editor I work in sends a heartbeat as I type — to a [wakapi](https://github.com/muety/wakapi)
server I run myself, on my own box, rather than to anyone else's. A nightly job folds
those heartbeats into hours per language, per project and per editor, going back to
August 2020, and the page can tell the hours that went through an AI assistant from the
ones that didn't. No login, nothing to sign up for, and no numbers I can quietly round up.
